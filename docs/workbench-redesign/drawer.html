Behaviour

  • Wide (≥ 1100 px): unchanged. Inspector docked on the right.
  • Narrow (< 1100 px): the inspector leaves the layout, so the view gets the room. A vertical edge tab on the right shows the live verdict (PASS / FAIL) so the answer is never hidden.
  • Click the tab or the top-bar Results button to slide the inspector over the view. Click outside it, or press Esc, to close. No state is lost: it is the same panel, only positioned differently.
  • Very narrow (< 700 px): the left inputs panel folds the same way, behind an Inputs tab.
  • Automatic: no setting and no toggle. Resizing the window switches modes live; it never reorders or hides content in wide mode.
