# Issue 46 — Phase 45: rational quadratic spline/circle deviation and coverage

PR #72 merged at `9977032f6d2caba5aa55c26c4a4546c288958106`, old branch deleted. Continuation: `feature/native-cad-spline-circle-deviation`.

`PlaneParameterMap::verify_spline_circle_deviation` binds source face/edge/p-curve/wrapper identities and requires an associated 3D CIRCLE. The complete active spline domain must consist of rational quadratic Bezier-separated knot spans: multiplicity at least two at both ends of each active degree-two span. General knot layouts/degrees refuse. No periodic wrapping or closure is inferred from declaration flags.

Each homogeneous control is mapped from raw plane UV to world millimetres and into the exact source circle frame with outward intervals. Bernstein degree-four product coefficients bound `X²+Y²−R²W²` over the entire span. Positive weights bound W below. Therefore radial error is bounded by the residual divided by `Wmin² Rmin`, since `|rho−R|=|rho²−R²|/(rho+R)` and `rho+R>=R`. Homogeneous Z controls bound out-of-plane error; their Euclidean combination bounds complete-span curve-to-circle distance. This retains polynomial correlations that an axis-aligned point box loses; no sampled-point acceptance test is used.

Optional full-circle coverage requires exactly equal endpoint Cartesian controls, nonzero projected radius, and one strictly consistent sign for every Bernstein coefficient of `X Y'−Y X'` across all spans. The closed continuous curve then has strictly positive or negative angular change: an integer number of full turns with nonzero magnitude. Every circle angle has a curve point within the same deviation bound, making it symmetric Hausdorff. This does **not** prove a single winding, simple trim, finite arc selection, or complete master/body validity. The report explicitly distinguishes unverified reverse coverage from verified whole-circle coverage and records angular orientation.

All 15 bracket spline associations (three spans each) pass full coverage at unchanged 1e-7 mm. Changed rational weights, stale documents, insufficient span budgets, invalid tolerances and cancellation through the final progress callback refuse atomically. A 1e-9 raw closure gap still passes one-sided deviation but refuses full coverage. A valid non-Bezier-separated quadratic remains evaluable and refuses this specialized certificate. Progress records completed/total spans; no partial report escapes.
