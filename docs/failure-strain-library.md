# Failure strain of every library material

Status: shipped. Every entry of the material library (17 curated built-ins + 1,375 MIL-HDBK-5J conditions) has a failure
strain for the finite-strain collapse (`docs/finite-strain-collapse.md`), in `mechanics-core/src/fracture.rs`, generated by
`tools/gen_fracture.py`. The Lug Analysis toolbox starts from it (Failure Strain row) and says in the Notes where the number
came from; a typed value overrides it.

## The rule

    eps_f = m * ln(1 + A)        A = the condition's own tabulated elongation (lowest of L / LT, else ST)
    floor 0.03, cap 0.50, flat default 0.10 when the condition has no elongation

`eps_f` is the equivalent plastic strain at the hole at which the lug is taken to fail under bearing. `m` is a family
multiplier and never exceeds 2.0, the largest value validated against pin-bearing tests.

| Family | m | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Aluminium 7075 | 1.0 | NACA TN 1503 bearing tests (validated) |
| Aluminium 2014, 2024 | 2.0 | NACA TN 1503 + TN 1502 bearing tests (validated); Bao and Wierzbicki 2004 (tensile fracture strain of 2024-T351, see below) |
| Other 2xxx aluminium | 2.0 | the validated 2014 / 2024 behaviour; not tested for the individual alloy |
| Other aluminium (5xxx, 6xxx, other 7xxx, cast), magnesium, beta titanium, cobalt alloys, copper alloys | 1.0 | no evidence: the lower bound `ln(1 + A)` |
| Low-alloy, intermediate, high-alloy, carbon steels | 2.0 | AMS 6414 4340 (260 ksi): RA >= 30 % at A = 10 %, true fracture strain / `ln(1 + A)` = 3.7, capped at 2.0 |
| PH stainless | 2.0 | AMS 5643 17-4PH: RA >= 40 % (H900) and 45 % (H1025) at A = 10-12 %, ratio 5.3, capped |
| Austenitic stainless, Fe-Cr-Ni (A-286, N-155) | 2.0 | annealed 304: RA 50-70 % at A 40-55 %, capped |
| Unalloyed, alpha, alpha-beta titanium | 2.0 | Ti-6Al-4V ASM data sheet: RA 25-36 % at A 10-14 %, ratio 3.0 and up, capped |
| Nickel-base alloys | 1.4 | Inconel 718 (AMS 5662 minimum, VDM sheet): RA 15 % at A 12 %, ratio 1.4 |

Entries per basis: 289 validated, 345 family (tensile-anchored, not bearing-validated), 328 lower bound, 403 flat default (no
elongation in the handbook for the condition), 16 typical (curated built-ins, elongation of the condition they stand for),
10 capped, 1 not applicable (carbon/epoxy).

## Validation against bearing tests (`lug-solver/tests/failure_strain_rule.rs`)

Pin 0.5 in, plate 0.25 x 2.0 in, edge distance e/D 1.5 and 2.0, finite-strain collapse at `eps_f = m ln(1 + A)` against the
measured ultimate bearing stress. Source data: R. L. Moore, NACA TN 1503 (1948) Tables I and III (six bars) and NACA TN 1502
(1948) Tables I and III (four plate / sheet specimens, with-grain). The TN 1502 points were not used to set `m`.

| Specimen | A | m | e/D 1.5 | e/D 2.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 75S-T 1x2 bar | 11.2 % | 1.0 | +1.7 % | -1.7 % |
| 75S-T 2x2 bar | 11.4 % | 1.0 | +1.8 % | +0.5 % |
| 24S-T 1x2 bar | 19.6 % | 2.0 | +1.7 % | +1.4 % |
| 24S-T 2x2 bar | 18.8 % | 2.0 | -2.2 % | -2.9 % |
| 14S-T 1x2 bar | 12.0 % | 2.0 | -2.1 % | -2.5 % |
| 14S-T 2x2 bar | 11.6 % | 2.0 | -0.2 % | +0.1 % |
| TN 1502 14S-T plate | 9.8 % | 2.0 | +2.8 % | -8.5 % |
| TN 1502 14S-W plate | 17.3 % | 2.0 | +1.4 % | -1.0 % |
| TN 1502 Alclad 14S-T | 11.5 % | 2.0 | -0.3 % | -5.3 % |
| TN 1502 Alclad 14S-W | 21.0 % | 2.0 | +0.2 % | -6.6 % |

Mean absolute error 2.1 %, range -8.5 to +2.8 %. The T and W tempers of 14S differ by a factor of two in elongation (10-12 %
against 17-21 %): the elongation scaling itself, not only the multiplier, is tested.

## Independent literature checks

* **Bao and Wierzbicki (2004), Int. J. Mech. Sci. 46, 81-98**, 2024-T351 fracture locus (equivalent strain at fracture against
  stress triaxiality eta): `0.1225 (eta + 1/3)^-0.46` for eta in -1/3..0, `1.9 eta^2 - 0.18 eta + 0.21` for 0..0.4, `0.15 / eta` for
  0.4..0.95, with the peak 0.45 at eta = 0.4. At the smooth-tensile-bar triaxiality eta = 1/3 the locus gives 0.36; the rule gives
  `2 ln(1 + 0.19) = 0.35` for A = 19 %. A lug bearing failure (shear-out through the ligament) lies at low-to-moderate triaxiality.
* **Ti-6Al-4V (annealed)**, MatWeb ASM data sheet: elongation 14 %, reduction of area 25-36 %, i.e. true fracture strain 0.29-0.45
  against `ln(1.14) = 0.13`: ratio above 2.2, so m = 2.0 is within the tensile evidence.
* **17-4PH** AMS 5643 (distributor data sheet): H900 A 10 %, RA >= 40 %; H1025 A 12 %, RA >= 45 %. **4340 at 260 ksi** AMS 6414 (E4340 /
  SSA data sheets): A 10 %, RA >= 30 %, typical 35 %. **Inconel 718** VDM data sheet (AMS 5662/5663 minima): A 12 %, RA 15 %.
  **Annealed 304**: RA 50-70 %, A 40-55 % (Online Metals, MakeItFrom).

## What this does not establish

* Outside 2014, 2024 and 7075 aluminium there are **no bearing-test data in the sources found**. The steel, PH stainless, titanium
  and nickel multipliers are anchored on tensile reduction of area (a published, reproducible quantity) and capped at the largest
  validated multiplier, but a pin-bearing failure strain of those alloys has not been checked against bearing tests. Their basis is
  `Family`, shown in the Notes.
* Several reduction-of-area values come from supplier/mill data sheets quoting AMS minima rather than from the ASM Handbook
  itself (not retrievable here); MMPDS / MIL-HDBK-5J tabulate elongation but not reduction of area.
* 403 conditions have no tabulated elongation and get the flat default 0.10 (the value that never over-predicted the NACA bars).
* Handbook bearing allowables (`Fbru`) were tried as a calibration target for every condition and **rejected**: they are thin-sheet
  design values (about 1.9-2.0 x Ftu at e/D 2.0) that the plane-strain bar model cannot reach for 482 of the 647 conditions it was
  tried on, and where it can (165 conditions) the strain it needs is inflated (7075: 0.19-0.30 against 0.11 from the thick-bar
  tests), so using it would over-predict thick lugs.
* The rejected calibration is kept as `lug-solver/examples/calibrate_failure_strain.rs` (it runs every condition; about 20 minutes).
* Strains above 0.4 are unvalidated (the NACA data reach 0.38); the cap is 0.50.
