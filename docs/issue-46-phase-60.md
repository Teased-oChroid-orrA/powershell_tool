# Issue 46 — Phase 60: sliver reconnection by 2-3 flips and edge removal

Stacked on Phase 59 (PR #87).

`fea-core::meshing::tet_flip::improve(topology, protected_faces, options)` reconnects low-quality tetrahedra of a validated `TetTopology` without moving a vertex. Operations: the 2-3 face flip and edge removal (the general n-to-2(n-2) reconnection, so the 3-2 and 4-4 flips are special cases), the latter choosing the best triangulation of the edge's link polygon by dynamic programming over the exact `orientation` predicate. An edge removal requires every link triangle to see the edge's two endpoints on the sides the old ring fixes (this, not mere positivity, rules out overlapping tiles); a 2-3 flip requires the three cones to agree (the segment between the apexes crosses the face). An operation is accepted only if the worst radius ratio among the affected cells improves by `min_gain`, so the passes terminate; cells are processed worst first and a cell touched in a pass is not reused until the next. Boundary faces, caller-protected faces and all edges lying on them are locked. The result is re-validated (`TetTopology::new`), and the boundary face set and total volume must be unchanged or the call errors.

`plc_conform::Options::reconnect` runs it over the extracted interior cells (their boundary is exactly the recovered sub-facets); the report carries the `FlipReport`. `step-inventory --conform-reconnect` exposes it.

Evidence: six reproducible 60-point Delaunay clouds in the unit cube (radius-ratio target 0.4): 182-208 low cells become 149-181 (about 15 % fewer) using 2-3 flips, 3-2/4-4/5-1... edge removals (up to six tetrahedra around the edge); boundary face set, vertex list and volume unchanged; deterministic; a second run never worsens the worst cell; protected faces survive; a zero target leaves the mesh untouched. On the 4x4x1 plate with a through hole the interior cells keep their boundary, volume (12) and per-sub-facet single use. `solid-kernel`: all pass with and without `--features occt`; `fea-core` meshing tests pass.

Limits: the worst cells of the random clouds sit next to the boundary (locked), so the minimum radius ratio often stays near 0.003-0.005; removing those needs Steiner points or vertex smoothing. Only interior edges with an ordinary closed ring of at most `max_link` (8) cells are removed. No claim is made for solver conditioning.
