# Issue 383, Phase 1: public tapped-hole specimen search

2026-11-27. Scope: find a public, physically tested directly tapped assembly that can independently validate pull-out load, load–displacement response, and failure mode. No FE result or analytical capacity was changed.

## Admission record

A capacity-validation specimen needs, for the same tested assembly: actual mating thread profile and dimensions, engaged length, parent wall and fixture geometry, bolt and parent true stress–plastic-strain curves from the tested material conditions, independent rupture calibration, force–displacement history, and observed failure mode. Missing measurements cannot be inferred by fitting the pull-out result. Published mean geometry or catalog material properties do not become specimen-level measurements.

## Sources screened

- [NASA TM-86539](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19860012479/downloads/19860012479.pdf): direct-tapped 1/4-28 UNJF aluminum-parent pull-outs. The existing Evidence tables view holds its published load results. The report does not give each specimen's actual engagement, thread profile, local wall and fixture geometry, material curves, or displacement history. Reject for capacity calibration.
- [Tipton and Daily, University of Tulsa](https://www.machinedesign.com/news/article/21829573/internal-thread-strip-out-tests-yield-surprising-results): 5/8-18 UNF threads in SAE 1026AK tube stock, with formed/cut thread comparisons and a fixture description. Its rounded formed-thread load trend is already in the Evidence tables view. Cross sections were examined, but specimen-level measured profiles and load–displacement records are not published. It supplies yield, ultimate strength and elongation, not a true stress–plastic-strain curve or fracture calibration. The article also gives conflicting maximum tap-hole diameters. Reject for capacity calibration; retain as a qualitative damage-trend check.
- [Wu et al., *Thin-Walled Structures* 163 (2021) 107653, author manuscript](https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/files/90086877/Authors_copy_Thin_Walled_Structures_Wu_et_al.pdf): physically tested M8×1.25 threads in 6082-T6 aluminum. One process uses conventional twist drilling and cutting taps, so it is a directly tapped comparison. The paper describes fixture, wall, bolt insertion, repeated strip-out tests, representative load–deflection response and group engagement statistics. It uses standard minimum parent properties rather than measured true stress–plastic-strain and fracture curves from the tested stock; individual thread profiles, engagement/force traces and bolt curves are absent. It is a promising group-level check, but cannot independently calibrate this contact failure measure. Metric geometry also lies outside the Workbench's inch-only UI; any later solver comparison must state its distinct applicability.
- [Zhang et al., *Materials* 19 (2026) 3070](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/19/14/3070): machine-screw specimens use hand-tapped holes in 6063-T6 aluminum RHS sections. The study reports repeated physical tests, local displacement instrumentation, modeled material response, fixture and wall dimensions, and both stripping and screw-fracture modes. Published results are group averages and selected curves. It does not establish specimen-level measured thread root/crest profiles, raw traces, same-heat coupon provenance for both material curves, or independent fracture calibration. The thin RHS wall bends and tears near the hole, so the current axisymmetric ring model is not a direct representation. Two result summaries also disagree on one specimen designation. Reject as a complete calibration specimen; retain as a potential future 3D external comparison after source reconciliation.
- [Bull et al., *Journal of Structural Fire Engineering* 6 (2015), author manuscript](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/89369/1/2_pdfsam_Tensile.pdf): measured threaded-assembly curves and material tests, but uses bolt-and-nut assemblies rather than tapped parent material. Exclude from direct-tapped calibration.

## Result and next search

No screened public source meets the full matched-specimen gate. Do not combine a pull-out series from one study with material curves or rupture parameters from another heat or assembly. The current 2D contact model remains an offline numerical diagnostic, and the Workbench's governing capacity remains analytical.

Next search should focus on public supplementary/raw files or dissertations that identify each tapped specimen and its coupon tests. If a complete record is found, verify every field against the original publication, digitize every used source table and plot in the editable Evidence tables feature, then reserve part of the specimens for held-out validation. Do not promote group means to specimen records. The previously noted local HTTP dependence of the Evidence tables page is a separate deferred change; this search does not alter it.
