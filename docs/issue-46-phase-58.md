# Issue 46 — Phase 58: incremental Delaunay engine and conforming PLC recovery

Stacked on Phase 57 (PR #85); the branch also carries `fea-core` changes.

**Engine.** `solid-kernel/src/delaunay3.rs` (crate-private) is an adjacency-linked incremental 3D Delaunay: visibility-walk location, connected conflict-cavity search with the exact `insphere`/`orient3d` predicates of `fea-core`'s reference mesher (cospherical ties join the cavity, with a strict-cavity retry if that is not star-shaped), cone re-triangulation and atomic failure. A point that rounding leaves just outside a hull plane replaces the hull faces it sees (the hull may become slightly non-convex); a point outside the hull and every conflicting tetrahedron is refused. `fea-core::meshing::tet_delaunay` gained a checked incremental `Delaunay` wrapper and `tet_topology` exact 2D `orientation_2d` / `in_circle_2d`; `tetrahedralize` now shares one `insert_index` implementation (unchanged behaviour, existing tests pass).

**Recovery.** `plc_conform::conform(vertices, facets, options, progress)` takes a closed manifold, outward-oriented triangulated shell and splits sub-segments (concentric-shell points at input vertices, midpoints elsewhere) and sub-facets (circumcentres of the per-facet planar Delaunay triangulations) until every sub-segment is a Delaunay edge and every sub-facet a Delaunay face, then extracts the enclosed cells. Encroachment is strict-inside with a 1e-9 margin (right angles and cocircular grids otherwise refine forever); an exact presence check after each queue drain forces anything a tie left out. Verification is independent of the heuristics: exact set membership, each sub-facet bounding the extracted cells exactly once, and the divergence-theorem volume to 1e-9. Steiner points are binary64 and lie on their parent segment/facet up to rounding (largest relative offset reported). `Options::protect_encroached = false` splits only absent items.

**Evidence.** A 2x2x1 box (conflicting face diagonals) needs 5 Steiner points and extracts the exact volume 4 with sub-facet area 16; a 4x4x1 plate with a 2x2 through-hole (18° input angles at the outer corners) is recovered in 0.01 s with the hole excluded and volume 12; invalid/open/inward shells, the Steiner budget (0 and 20 on a 400:1 graded plate) and cancellation refuse. `Delaunay3` is checked against the reference mesher (volume, empty circumspheres, boundary count), including on-face/on-edge points, a point one ulp above a hull plane, a far-outside refusal and the cell budget. `solid-kernel`: 200 passed with `--features occt`, 183 with `--no-default-features`; `fea-core` meshing 54 passed; 0 failed.

**Limit found (not fixed).** On the NEMA17 bracket's 469-vertex / 978-facet sampled PLC the recovery does not converge: 416 of 1467 segments are encroached at the start, and the cascade grows without bound for both the encroachment rule (pending items rise past 6000 at 18 600 Steiner points) and the missing-only rule (pending items rise past 10 000 at 11 000 points, then a circumcentre leaves its facet). Every triangle edge is protected, and surface meshes from chart CDTs have many acute and skinny triangles, so classical Delaunay refinement has no termination guarantee here. The default budget (4000) makes it refuse in about 2 s with a `Limit` error; `step-inventory --surface --conform [--conform-max-steiner N] [--conform-missing-only]` reproduces it. A usable path needs boundary recovery by local reconnection (flips / cavity re-tetrahedralization as in TetGen) or isotropic remeshing of the surface first. No quality or sliver bound is established for the recovered cells.
