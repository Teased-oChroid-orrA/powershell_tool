# Issue 46: topology-aware boss-rim repair plan

Status: research and execution plan, 2026-10-23. No mesher change or retirement approval is implied.

## Objective and baseline

Remove the short boundary edges created when two lattice vertices snap toward the plate/boss junction, while preserving the surface and the curved-body convergence established before Phase 136. Then rerun the same-STEP OCCT/native quality and static-solve comparison. The production backend stays OCCT and `occt_retirement_approved` stays false.

Phase 134 measured initially regular 0.166667 mm lattice edges becoming roughly 0.008 mm at the rim. After repair, the default-size plate-with-boss still has 6 OCCT and 20 native tets below the 0.1 scaled-Jacobian gate. Phase 135's connected synthetic plate/boss had 27 sub-gate tets after repair, with orientation, link, quality and fidelity refusals. Phase 136's snap restriction cleared the parity-table boss gate but multiplied Tet10 curved-cylinder radial error by about 6 at coarse size and 19 at fine size. That restriction was reverted. These are baselines to reproduce, not values to encode as permanent exact counts.

The existing path is `solid-kernel::mesh_volume` -> `fea-core::tetmesh::mesh_solid_reported` (lattice, boundary snap, reconnect) -> opt-in `meshing::boundary_repair::repair` -> named surfaces. `boundary_repair::Work::collapse` already checks boundary-edge membership, the link condition, positive orientation, local quality, and changed-face fidelity, with rollback. `cleanup_tet` already tries interior flips and edge removal before collapse. Extend this owner rather than adding a second snap policy or a new mesher. Generic `TetOptions::repair` remains off; CAD `mesh_volume` opts in.

## Research and design choice

- [CGAL tetrahedral remeshing](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Tetrahedral_remeshing/index.html) treats feature curves and surface patches as a topological complex and combines local edge operations with vertex relocation while preserving that complex. This supports explicit feature classification and a guarded local transaction; it does not provide a drop-in Rust solution here. Its [reference](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Tetrahedral_remeshing/group__PkgTetrahedralRemeshingRef.html) is GPL, so do not copy code or introduce it as a runtime dependency.
- [Ghadyani et al., boundary recovery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2841449/) describes local cavity retriangulation for compromised tet quality or missing boundary facets. Use it as a fallback design direction only if the existing flip/collapse machinery cannot clear the measured link obstruction without changing the surface complex.

Preferred approach: identify the short edge and the two incident CAD faces at the actual rim, move the intended survivor to their intersection, and attempt a small, rollback-protected sequence of existing local topology operations followed by collapse. Recompute local face ownership and surface error after the transaction. First prove which precise link obstruction occurs on the connected regression; do not assume any arbitrary flip repairs it. Avoid global minimum-edge or snap suppression rules, which leave a vertex off the surface or repeat Phase 136's curved-body regression. A broader pre-snap boundary discretization change would have a much larger blast radius and is the fallback only if bounded local operations fail.

## Implementation sequence

1. **Reproduce and freeze the evidence.** On a short-lived branch from the current `main`, check status and free disk, preserve the three untracked settings/recovery files, and use only this checkout's Cargo target with one Cargo process at a time. Add a connected plate `[0,0,0]`–`[12,4,1]` plus radius-1.2 boss centered at `[6,2]` through `z=4` to `fea-core/tests/tetmesh_repair.rs` using exact SDF and distinct plate, wall and cap `FaceHit`s (`base=0.6`, `boundary_size=0.25`). Capture pre/post short edges, failed collapse reason, gate count, surface distance, face areas, volume, orientation and time. A small direct `Work` test should isolate the observed failed link condition. Keep the normal suite quick; mark the 180k-tet full case ignored if necessary and retain a smaller connected scale as a regular regression if it reproduces the same obstruction.
2. **Repair the local cavity in `fea-core/src/meshing/boundary_repair.rs`.** Add the narrow operation to the existing `cleanup_tet` path after simple flips fail and before splitting/relaxation. Feature eligibility must come from two distinct adjacent face hits with independent normals and bounded projection to their intersection. Try only connectivity changes within the two endpoint stars; commit only when the link condition, exact positive orientation, distinct cells, boundary manifold, local quality, unchanged face identities, signed-distance/other-face fidelity and existing volume/growth limits all hold. Use the existing mark/rollback mechanism for the entire sequence. If a needed boundary flip is not supported by existing operations, implement one bounded operation there with a focused topology test, or stop and document why a cavity retriangulation is required. Do not relax quality gates or numerical tolerances.
3. **Prove geometry and numerical preservation.** Extend the connected boss test to require zero elements under `QualityThresholds::default()` on the repaired target, positive orientation, conforming/manifold boundary, unchanged named faces, bounded per-face area and enclosed volume, no increase in boundary-to-CAD error, and deterministic output. Retain sphere/cylinder/torus repair tests, Tet10 validity, and `high_order_convergence` at both cylinder sizes. Compare Tet4/Tet10 RMS and maximum radial errors against an unmodified baseline at the exact same settings; reject a statistically or numerically material increase even if the existing ordering assertion passes. Keep the fillet-rim tagging test. Check both feature-hit and no-face-hit paths, plus a sharp corner and a tangent seam, so feature classification does not spread to smooth curves.
4. **Focused same-STEP parity first.** Run release `fea-problem/tests/occt_vs_native.rs` with `CASE='plate with a boss'`, then native box-plus-boss and boss-plus-hole cases, with `NO_SOLVE=1` for fast diagnosis followed by actual solves. Both paths must pass the default full quality gate and solve; record min scaled Jacobian, radius and edge ratios, size jump, sub-gate count, elements/DOFs, named load area, reaction/equilibrium, displacement, energy and elapsed mesh/solve time. Use the same STEP text, element size and build for both paths. The existing `table` test only prints; its exit status is not a gate. Add focused assertions or evaluate and record every field explicitly.
5. **Full parity and qualification.** If focused gates pass, run the entire 19-model release table with actual solves and the existing asserted parity test. Compare all 38 rows with the Phase 128/most recent pre-change baseline; list every residual gate failure, including the native tilted hole and other shared cases, without claiming this repair resolves them. Require no model's quality, CAD fidelity or solver result to regress; investigate a real regression before accepting the increment. Timing is noisy, so repeat any apparent efficiency regression under serial, comparable runs before concluding. Run `cargo test -p fea-core --no-default-features --locked --offline -j1`, `cargo test -p solid-kernel --no-default-features --locked --offline -j1`, `cargo test -p solid-kernel --features occt --locked --offline -j1`, `cargo test -p fea-problem --features occt --locked --offline -j1`, and `cargo check -p app-tui --features occt --lib --tests --locked --offline -j1` as applicable to touched code. If OCCT is unavailable, record that parity remains unqualified; do not substitute feature-off results.
6. **Integration evidence.** Re-query CodeGraph dependents after editing; inspect the diff and `git diff --check`. Record exact commit, platform, commands, counts, geometry/quality/solve measurements, timing conditions and limits in a new append-only Phase 137 note. Advance `docs/issue-46-current.json`, readiness/resume links and checklist only to the evidenced scope. Use the repository's authorized forward-only branch/PR/plain-push/squash-merge flow after qualification. Do not delete historical docs, remove OCCT, or set `occt_retirement_approved` without separate owner approval.

## Stop or redesign conditions

Stop the candidate and retain the baseline if the connected boss gate only clears by leaving a boundary vertex off its CAD face; a link violation, inverted or non-manifold cavity is needed; named face/load area drifts beyond the existing physical error bounds; curved-body Tet10 convergence or fillet tagging regresses; or the full parity run exposes a new model failure. A bounded negative result should be documented with the exact counterexample and the next cavity strategy, rather than weakened thresholds.
