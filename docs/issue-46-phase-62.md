# Issue 46 — Phase 62: validated metric tensor fields (#57)

Stacked on Phase 61 (PR #88). New module `fea-core::meshing::metric`; nothing in it meshes — it builds and checks the field a mesher would follow (the anisotropic adaptation of #58 consumes it later).

- **Validated SPD metrics.** `Metric::new` refuses non-finite, asymmetric (relative 1e-10) and non-positive-definite matrices with a typed reason; `isotropic(h)` and `from_sizes(orthonormal directions, sizes)` build them; `validate(&MetricBounds)` enforces per-direction min/max size and a maximum anisotropy ratio; `validate_field` names the failing vertex.
- **Metric-space evaluation.** `length(e) = sqrt(e^T M e)`, `density() = sqrt(det M)`, and `edge_length` between two end metrics (logarithmic mean of the end lengths, the exact integral for sizes varying geometrically along the edge; checked against a 20 000-point numerical integral to 1e-6).
- **Interpolation** is log-Euclidean (SPD-preserving, symmetric in its arguments, geometric mean of commuting sizes). **Composition** is intersection by simultaneous reduction (`A^{1/2} Q max(1, D) Q^T A^{1/2}`): commutative, idempotent, dominates both operands and equals the larger of the two exactly along the reduction directions (tested on random rotated metrics).
- **Gradation** (`gradate`): Alauzet's bounded growth along mesh edges, intersecting each end with the other end's metric grown by `1 + beta l`; only ever shrinks sizes; matches the isotropic closed form `h_next <= h + beta d` and leaves graded fields untouched.
- **Geometry-driven sizes** (`curvature_metric`): chord deviation gives `sqrt(8 deviation / kappa)` per principal direction (a sphere of radius 5 at 0.01 gives 0.632...), flat directions and the normal take the maximum size, clamped to the bounds and the anisotropy limit.
- **AMR error fields** (`sizes_from_error`, `vertex_metrics_from_element_sizes`): only usable, non-negative (marking) `ErrorField`s drive `h_new = h (target/e)^(1/order)`; failed, unsupported and signed fields are refused with a reason rather than skipped, zero error asks for the maximum size, element sizes become vertex metrics by geometric mean.
- **Complexity and budget** (`tet_complexity`, `rescale_to_budget`): expected tetrahedra = sum of element volume times mean vertex density over the unit-edge tetrahedron volume sqrt(2)/12 (closed form `V / (h^3 V_unit)` verified); one size factor is iterated to the target and the result reports `met = false` when the size bounds bind.

Evidence: 12 module tests, all analytic or property checks; `fea-core` full suite 455 passed before this module, meshing tests pass. Not claimed: that any mesher follows a field, that complexity predicts the realised element count of a particular mesher, or 2D/surface metrics (3x3 only).
